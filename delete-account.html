Delete your account and data

SideWaysSaga 2025 (com.SANGames.SideWaysSaga2025) — SANGames

This page explains how to delete your SideWaysSaga 2025 account and all data associated with it. There are two ways to do it.

Option 1 — Delete it yourself, in the game (fastest)

  1. Open SideWaysSaga 2025 and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to the account menu in the main menu.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

Your account and data are removed immediately. This cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Ask us to delete it

If you can no longer sign in, send an email to sasorigx123@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion", and include:

  • the email address your account is registered with;
  • your in-game username.

The request must be sent from the email address registered to the account, so that we can confirm it is really yours. Requests are processed within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials (Firebase Authentication).
  • Your email address.
  • Your username, and the reservation that holds it — after deletion, the username becomes available to other players again.
  • Your game progress: in-game currency, owned vehicles, upgrades, customization and licence plates (Cloud Firestore).
  • Your leaderboard entry, including your score.

What may be kept, and for how long

Some records may be retained temporarily where we are required to do so by law, or to prevent fraud and abuse. Anything retained for those reasons is deleted once the reason no longer applies.

Data held by third-party providers used by the game — Google AdMob, Google Play Services, Unity and Photon — is governed by their own retention policies. See the privacy policy for the full list and links.

Questions

Write to sasorigx123@gmail.com.
