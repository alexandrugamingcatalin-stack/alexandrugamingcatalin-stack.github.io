What information does the Application collect?

Information you provide directly. When you create an account and sign in, the Application collects:

  • Email address — used to create and authenticate your account, and to allow password resets.
  • Password — handled by Firebase Authentication. The Service Provider never sees your password.
  • Username — chosen by you. Please note that your username is public: it is displayed to other players in the in-game leaderboard and in online multiplayer sessions.

Information generated by your use of the Application.

  • User ID — a unique identifier assigned to your account, used to store and retrieve your data.
  • Game progress data — such as your in-game currency, owned vehicles, upgrades, customization choices and licence plates.
  • Leaderboard score — your drift score, together with your username, is publicly readable by other players.

Information collected automatically.

  • Advertising ID — a resettable identifier provided by your device, used by Google AdMob to serve and measure advertisements.
  • Device and connection information — such as device model, operating system version, language and IP address, collected by the third-party services listed below in order to deliver ads, run online multiplayer and diagnose technical problems.

The Application does not collect your precise or approximate geographic location, your real name, your date of birth, your gender, your phone number or your contacts. It requests only the INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions.

How is your information used?

  • To create, authenticate and secure your account.
  • To save your game progress and make it available across sessions and devices.
  • To operate public leaderboards and online multiplayer sessions.
  • To display advertisements, which fund the development of the Application.
  • To detect and fix technical problems and prevent abuse.

Legal bases for processing (GDPR)

  • Contract — account creation, authentication, saving your progress, leaderboards and multiplayer. Without this data the Application cannot provide these features.
  • Consent — personalised advertising and the associated use of your Advertising ID. You are asked for consent when you first open the Application, and you may change or withdraw it at any time from the in-game settings, under "Privacy options".
  • Legitimate interests — security, abuse prevention and diagnosing technical faults.
